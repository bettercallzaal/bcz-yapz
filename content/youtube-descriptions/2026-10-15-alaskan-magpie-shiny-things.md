BCZ YapZ Episode 25 w/ Alaskan Magpie from Shiny Things

I sat down with Alaskan Magpie (solo founder of Shiny Things) to talk browser games built around your time. He spent years in web3 gaming partnerships before going solo in February, and his studio is about eight months old.

Magpie explains why he went solo once AI tools let an idea become a prototype in an afternoon, and why everything he builds respects your time: if you can play, great, and if you can't, AI can play parts of it for you. He demos Fable Boy, a retro console in any browser with 30 games, nine console generations already built and a path for creators to make their own cartridges. Then he walks through Waypoint, where you sign in and get a full solar system in 30 seconds, hire mercenary fleets, send AI crews on missions and open trade routes with other players. He also shows Doctrine, a card RTS, and the golden cartridge scavenger hunt that ties Waypoint and Fable Boy together.

Open, buildable games like these are what I want the next ZABAL Gamez to lean on, where people explore other creators' builds and add on top. Magpie is offering free arcade cabinets to other builders, and a game night together is next.
