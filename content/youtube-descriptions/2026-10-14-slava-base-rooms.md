BCZ YapZ Episode 24 w/ Slava from Base Rooms

I sat down with Slava (builder of Base Rooms) to talk Base Rooms, Buddies and on-chain art. Slava is a longtime crypto tinkerer and former audio producer who ran a studio in New York City, and after he has helped so many builders, it was fun to flip the chair.

Slava shares how he went from mining to NFTs and permanent storage, then into building with agentic tools at buildathons with Base and Coinbase. He explains why he keeps building at night as a solo dev and a dad of two young kids, and how Buddies brought inscriptions to Base with the code stored on chain and an open license, so any client that indexes the chain can show them. We talk about what publishing on chain does to a creator's bundle of rights and why licensing matters for music. Then we tour the Base Rooms desktop: we listen to music together, open the art gallery that only shows NFTs you own, and chat with a Buddy agent that flags risky transactions.

This is the kind of open, buildable primitive The ZAO wants to build on. We talked about adding a WaveZStation player with song previews inside Base Rooms, and you can find Slava at baserooms.io.
