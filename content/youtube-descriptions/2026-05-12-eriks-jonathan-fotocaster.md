BCZ YapZ Episode 20 w/ Erik and Jonathan from Fotocaster

I sat down with Erik and Jonathan (the builders behind Fotocaster) to talk putting photography on chain. Both came to Farcaster as photographers - Erik as a nomadic investor who picked up a camera, and Jonathan (JC) as a working photographer and former co-host of I Took A Photo.

Erik shares how he used Farcaster as a photo journal from 2023, and JC walks through going from the Nature Channel to writing Distribution Is Hard and building Founder Check. They explain the thesis behind Fotocaster: separate putting something on chain from selling it, because not everything needs to be a marketplace. I mint a photo live in the mini app and we go through the edition types, from open editions on a price curve to self-mints, collector-only drops and timed editions. Erik shares the two-week numbers - 371 creators, 787 collectors and 4,000 photos collected - and we talk about moving storage from IPFS to Arweave.

This one matters for anyone in The ZAO putting creative work out there. Artists keep 99% on Fotocaster and 1% goes back to fund free on-chain deployment, a public-good model the two of them are still tuning so it can sustain itself.
